# Implemented scope and limits · 0.3.0

RosterCore is runnable for local evaluation. Schema-1 scenarios remain accepted; explicit migration adds schema-3 catalogues while preserving IDs and history. See `EVOLUTION.md` for the evolution contract and `TEST_REPORT.md` for executed evidence.

Implemented: dated automatic work-pattern rules with explicit manual precedence and traceable core previews; guided catalogue editing and accessible outcome notifications; ISO week profiles with manual/recurring/solver-selected classification and hard/desired frequencies; offline versioned Italian holidays and closures; dated employment, typed/default attributes and predicate selectors; versioned work patterns; logical shifts and actual variants; responsibility and interval-presence coverage with lexicographic substitutes; cross-day sequences and minimum elapsed rest; cumulative rational COUNT fairness across profiles and workforce changes; independent verification; history/publication protection and partial regeneration. The bilingual local playground edits these declarations, previews core-resolved hours/profiles, imports AI/HR documents and shows operational substitutions and differences.

Explicit limits:

- Elapsed-time rules support UTC and the embedded Europe/Rome IANA transitions for 2000–2100. Other timezone labels remain usable for civil COUNT scheduling, but are rejected for MIN_REST. Ambiguous times need an explicit fold; nonexistent local times fail validation. The Italian holiday catalogue supports 2001–2200. Catalogue/timezone data require maintained releases when the underlying rules change.
- Fairness measures assignments, not minutes or burden points. EARLIEST_BALANCE and operational partial-day leave remain unsupported. No minimum-rest, maximum-hours or labor-contract rule is implied; configure the rules required by the host. Predicate NOT is supported; constraint-AST NOT/IMPLIES are not.
- REMAINING must be directly inside a flat AND branch with COUNT siblings using identical selectors. Those branches may appear inside nested AND/OR trees. Ambiguous forms are rejected.
- Coverage uses fixed interval change points, not continuous shift-start optimization. A person can cover multiple requirements unless an explicit exclusiveGroup prevents it. A bound responsibility requires every matching shift to serve a qualified declared duty; disable bindAssignments explicitly for a pure lower-bound requirement.
- Exact rational LCM scaling can reject large/changing denominators or very large historical totals. The three-year horizon and resource caps are admission limits, not performance guarantees. Large models can return FEASIBLE/UNKNOWN when their budget expires. Preparation and verification are outside CP-SAT's interruptible search.
- Retain workforce IDs, dated arrangements and variant definitions used by historical assignments. Historical-context rebuilding cannot infer missing past data. Regeneration freezes the old prefix; a conflicting effective change is rejected or infeasible rather than rewriting protected work. Host systems own durable versions and the administrative treatment of closures/leave.
- Playground persistence uses browser local storage and explicit JSON exports. Undo is a bounded session edit stack; import/consolidation are explicit operations. There is no multi-user authentication, database, live HR-provider connection or public HTTP deployment. Reloaded scenarios survive; solved-result comparison is session-local. Deep trees and bulk exchange remain available in advanced JSON mode.
- Infeasibility assumption sets are sufficient and need not be minimal. The verifier independently checks feasibility and accounting; CP-SAT phase statuses supply optimality evidence.

Native platform results and measured examples are recorded in `TEST_REPORT.md`. Final operational acceptance remains with the user.

Automatic rules do not imply a mandatory arrangement: no match preserves unrestricted legacy behavior and is identified in the preview. Keep effective rule/pattern history when editing future configurations. Variant applicability previews cover up to 14 days per request; assignment preview supports a selected date and may show separate weekly-profile alternatives. Local progress indicators establish local coherence only, not global feasibility. Notifications display three at once with a bounded eight-entry queue; excess distinct errors are grouped with their full diagnostics and remain dismissible. Preview/important warning actions remain available until dismissed; ordinary successes auto-dismiss after about five seconds (paused during interaction).
